# Security policy

If you believe you have found a security issue in Sineclave or in
sineclave.com, please write to **info@marant.hr** with the subject **"Security report"**.

- We acknowledge reports within three working days.
- Please give us 90 days to fix the issue before public disclosure; we will keep you informed.
- We will not pursue legal action against good-faith research that avoids privacy violations,
  destruction of data and disruption of service, and that stays within what is needed to show the issue.
- Please do not test against production systems you do not own; ask us for a test environment.

Machine-readable contact: https://sineclave.com/.well-known/security.txt
